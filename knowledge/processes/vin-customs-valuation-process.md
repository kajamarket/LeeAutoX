# Automated VIN Valuation & Port Clearance Workflow

The statutory clearance protocol required by the Nigeria Customs Service for imported vehicles discharged at Lagos container terminals.

## Workflow Phases

1. **Manifest & Document Verification**: Authenticates Bill of Lading, export ownership title slips, and commercial invoices against the shipping line manifest.
2. **VIN Electronic Assessment**: Inputting the 17-character VIN into the Nigeria Customs Direct Trader Input (DTI) portal generates the standard CIF valuation benchmark and prevents arbitrary valuation adjustments.
3. **Assessment Notice Generation**: The system calculates the statutory tariff breakdown (CET duty, surcharge, ETLS, CISS, and VAT).
4. **Bank Duty Remittance**: Duty payments are remitted via designated commercial banks to the Federal Government Single Treasury Account (TSA).
5. **Physical Inspection / Scanning**: Port customs examiners verify chassis numbers and confirm vehicle parameters against the Single Goods Declaration (SGD).
6. **Terminal Delivery Order (TDO) Settlement**: Clearing agents settle terminal handling and forklift unstowing fees with terminal operators (APM Terminals, Grimaldi PTML).
7. **Gate Release**: Customs exit officers scan the gate pass and release the vehicle for final delivery.
