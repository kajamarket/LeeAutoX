# Four-Stage Vehicle Import Lifecycle

The standard operational workflow managing vehicle procurement from initial auction search to final port release and doorstep delivery.

```
[Auction Run-List Sourcing]
           │
           ▼
[On-Site Physical Inspection] ──► (Reject if structural damage/fraudulent codes)
           │
           ▼
[Live Broker Bidding & Win]
           │
           ▼
[Flatbed Transport to Scarborough Depot]
           │
           ▼
[Battery/Fluid Prep & 4-Car Container Loading]
           │
           ▼
[Rail to Montreal/Halifax & 25-35 Day Ocean Voyage]
           │
           ▼
[Pre-Arrival VIN Valuation & SGD Customs Lodgment in Lagos]
           │
           ▼
[Customs Duty Settlement & Terminal Delivery Release]
           │
           ▼
[Doorstep Handover or Inland Delivery to Abuja/PH]
```
