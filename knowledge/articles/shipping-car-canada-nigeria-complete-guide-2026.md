# Shipping a Car from Canada to Nigeria: 2026 Guide

An operational breakdown comparing RoRo and 40ft container freight rates from Montreal and Halifax to Lagos (Apapa and Tin Can Island), covering ocean transit schedules, terminal handling fees, and clearing workflows.

## Key Freight Points

- **Ocean Transit**: 25 to 35 days on water from Montreal/Halifax to Lagos.
- **RoRo Rates**: Range from $1,850 for sedans to $2,950 for large trucks.
- **Container Consolidation**: Costs $2,200 to $2,800 per vehicle slot while permitting the transport of spare parts and providing higher physical security.
