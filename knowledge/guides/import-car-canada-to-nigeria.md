# Importing Cars from Canada to Nigeria: Full Guide

A comprehensive operational manual guiding vehicle buyers through auction bidding, physical appraisal, Canadian export compliance, ocean freight, and customs clearance at Lagos ports.

## Key Sections Covered

1. **Wholesale Auction Access**: Navigating dealer-only platforms (Copart, ADESA, Impact) via an accredited broker.
2. **On-Site Inspection Protocols**: Identifying concealed structural issues, frame warping, and odometer rollbacks.
3. **Consolidation Economics**: Comparing single-unit RoRo versus consolidated 40ft container loading at Scarborough.
4. **Customs Valuation Rules**: Applying the 20% Common External Tariff (CET), 7% surcharge, 0.5% ETLS, and 7.5% VAT against official CIF values.
5. **Age Restrictions**: Ensuring compliance with Nigeria Customs age regulations prohibiting cars manufactured more than 12 to 15 years prior to arrival.
