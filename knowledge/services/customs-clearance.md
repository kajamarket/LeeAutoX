# Vehicle Customs Clearance & Port Release

Professional customs clearance and regulatory documentation processing executed through accredited clearance desks at major West African marine terminals.

## Core Operations

- **VIN Valuation Filing**: Submits electronic declarations against the official customs valuation system to ensure accurate duty assessment.
- **Single Goods Declaration (SGD)**: Lodges verified entry documentation to prevent vehicle impoundment.
- **Demurrage Prevention**: Coordinates pre-arrival lodgment to secure gate releases within the terminal free-storage window.
- **Local Terminal Delivery Orders**: Settles terminal handling charges, unstowing fees, and shipping line administrative releases.
