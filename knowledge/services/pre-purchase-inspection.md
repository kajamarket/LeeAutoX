# Pre-Purchase 150-Point Lot Inspection

Physical on-site vehicle appraisal conducted at Canadian auction yards prior to live auction bidding to eliminate the financial risks of digital-only bidding.

## Technical Inspection Scope

- **Diagnostic Scanning**: Evaluates the vehicle's OBD2 system for cleared trouble codes and emissions monitor readiness.
- **Engine & Mechanical Verification**: Tests engine turnover, detects fluid contamination (such as milky oil indicating head gasket failure), and checks belt conditions.
- **Structural Integrity**: Inspects frame rails, pinch welds, and unibody pull marks to detect undeclared collision damage.
- **Flood & Waterline Checks**: Examines seat track bolts, dash wiring harnesses, and seatbelt reels for silt or corrosion indicating flood exposure.
