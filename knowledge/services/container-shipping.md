# Ocean Containerization & Marine Freight

Comprehensive marine transportation and container logistics managing vehicle exports from Canadian loading terminals to West African destination berths.

## Operational Modes

1. **40ft High-Cube Multi-Car Consolidation**: Up to four vehicles are packed, wheel-strapped, and timber-braced inside a dedicated steel container at the Scarborough depot.
2. **Roll-on/Roll-off (RoRo)**: Drivable single vehicles are loaded onto specialized multi-tier vehicle carriers.
3. **Parts Stowage Allowance**: Containerized shipping permits consolidating genuine replacement parts and accessories alongside the vehicle at zero additional freight charge.
