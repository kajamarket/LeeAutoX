# Milestone Installment Payment Plans

A transparent 4-stage financing structure enabling vehicle importers to fund international procurement incrementally across verified delivery milestones.

## Milestone Schedule

1. **Stage 1: Commitment Deposit**: Initial bidding retainer activating targeted auction search and physical lot inspection.
2. **Stage 2: Auction Settlement**: Transferred upon winning the vehicle hammer bid, covering auction lot settlement and inland towing.
3. **Stage 3: Shipping & Containerization**: Paid once the vehicle is received at the Scarborough depot, photographed, strapped, and sealed into an ocean container.
4. **Stage 4: Customs Clearance & Handover**: Paid upon vessel discharge in Lagos or Tema to cover customs duty, port terminal charges, and vehicle release.
