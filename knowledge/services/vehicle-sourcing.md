# Vehicle Sourcing & Auction Bidding

LeeAutoX acts as a licensed purchasing broker on behalf of individual buyers, private fleets, and automotive dealerships seeking to procure vehicles directly from North American wholesale dealer auctions.

## Capabilities

- **Dealer-Only Auction Entry**: Grants access to closed commercial auctions in Ontario and across Canada without requiring the client to maintain an independent dealer license.
- **Run-List Curation**: Pre-screens daily run-lists to identify clean-title lease returns, bank repossessions, and lightly repairable salvage units.
- **Auction Pre-Bid Valuation**: Calculates strict bidding ceilings incorporating auction fees, inland transport, shipping freight, and destination customs duties.
