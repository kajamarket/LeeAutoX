# Scarborough Logistics Depot

Located at 109 Slan Ave, Scarborough, Ontario, this facility is LeeAutoX's centralized vehicle consolidation, prep, and container stuffing hub.

## Depot Functions

- **Receiving Lot**: Flatbed haulers deliver won auction vehicles within 24 to 48 hours of sale to avoid auction storage fees.
- **Ocean Prep**: Disconnects vehicle batteries, verifies fluid containment, and checks tire pressures.
- **Container Loading**: Uses timber bracing and heavy-duty straps to load up to four vehicles into 40ft high-cube ocean containers.
