# Canada Operations Hub

Canada serves as the sourcing and export foundation for LeeAutoX. Operations leverage Ontario's wholesale dealer network, commercial fleet leasing liquidations, and major ocean freight terminals.

## Key Responsibilities

- Sourcing across Copart, ADESA, and Impact Auto Auctions in Southern Ontario.
- Mechanical inspection and OBD2 diagnostic verification.
- Central containerization and vehicle loading at the Scarborough depot.
- Export declaration filing via the Canadian Export Reporting System (CERS) and CBSA vehicle title stamping.
