# LeeAutoX

LeeAutoX is an accredited international automotive brokerage and marine logistics firm incorporated in Ontario, Canada (Corporation No: 1761065-3), with active operational distribution hubs in Lagos, Nigeria, and Accra/Tema, Ghana.

## Core Mandate

The company provides cross-border auto procurement services that bridge wholesale North American dealer auto auctions (such as Copart, ADESA, and Impact Auto Auctions) with retail, commercial, and dealership buyers in West Africa.

## Key Operational Pillars

1. **Licensed Broker Access**: Leverages commercial dealer licenses to bid on restricted wholesale auctions across Ontario and North America.
2. **On-Site Lot Verification**: Deploys technicians to inspect prospective vehicles physically in Canadian yards before placing client bids.
3. **Dedicated Containerization**: Consolidates vehicles at its Scarborough loading depot into 40-foot high-cube containers to lower unit shipping costs and protect cargo.
4. **Direct Port Clearing**: Coordinates clearance through accredited clearing desks at Apapa, Tin Can Island, and Tema ports using official customs VIN valuation portals.
5. **Milestone Financing**: Eliminates full upfront capital exposure by offering structured 4-stage installment payments mapped to logistics milestones.
